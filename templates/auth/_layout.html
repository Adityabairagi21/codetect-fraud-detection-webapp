CoDetect

Financial fraud detection with anomaly feature detection

Two Isolation Forest models score every transaction: one on transaction characteristics alone, one enhanced with relationship features from the transaction graph. Each flagged transaction shows the features that made it unusual.

  • Graph features: counterparties, degrees, activity and volume in the last 24 hours
  • Low / Medium / High / Critical risk levels from documented percentile cut-points
  • Anomaly scores show how unusual a transaction is. They are not proof of fraud.
